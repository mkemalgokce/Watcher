OVERVIEW: List Android devices, copy the root certificate to one, or point its proxy at Watcher.

USAGE: watcher-cli android [<action>] [--serial <serial>] [--port <port>]

ARGUMENTS:
  <action>                list | cert (copy to Downloads) | proxy (set) | unproxy (clear) (default:
                          list)

OPTIONS:
  --serial <serial>       adb serial; defaults to the only connected device.
  --port <port>           Watcher port the device should use (default: from settings, 9090).
  -h, --help              Show help information.
