OVERVIEW: Switch a debugging tool on or off.

USAGE: watcher-cli tool <tool> <state>

ARGUMENTS:
  <tool>                  One of: block-list, allow-list, map-local, map-remote, breakpoint,
                          scripting, dns-spoofing, reverse-proxy, network-condition, no-caching,
                          external-proxy. (values: block-list, allow-list, map-local, map-remote,
                          breakpoint, scripting, dns-spoofing, reverse-proxy, network-condition,
                          no-caching, external-proxy)
  <state>

OPTIONS:
  -h, --help              Show help information.
