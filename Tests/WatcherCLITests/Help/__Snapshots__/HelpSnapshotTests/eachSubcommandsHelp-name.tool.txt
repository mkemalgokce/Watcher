OVERVIEW: Switch a debugging tool on or off.

USAGE: watcher-cli tool <tool> <state>

ARGUMENTS:
  <tool>                  One of: breakpoint, map-local, map-remote, scripting, block-list,
                          allow-list, reverse-proxy, network-condition, no-caching, dns-spoofing,
                          external-proxy. (values: breakpoint, map-local, map-remote, scripting,
                          block-list, allow-list, reverse-proxy, network-condition, no-caching,
                          dns-spoofing, external-proxy)
  <state>

OPTIONS:
  -h, --help              Show help information.
